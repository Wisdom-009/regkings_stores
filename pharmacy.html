Important Medicine Safety Tips

✓ Use medicines according to the directions of a qualified healthcare professional.
✓ Check the label, expiry date, and storage instructions before use.
✓ Do not share prescription medicines with other people.
✓ Keep medicines out of the reach of children.
✓ Seek professional advice if you experience unexpected side effects or are unsure how to use a medicine.
Care Comes First

Make Informed Healthcare Decisions.

Every person's healthcare needs are different. Avoid self-diagnosis and do not take prescription medicines without appropriate medical advice. A qualified healthcare professional can help you understand your options and determine when further medical attention is needed.
