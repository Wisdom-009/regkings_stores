Customer Information

Medicine Safety Should Always Come First.

Medicines can have side effects and may interact with other medicines or existing health conditions. If you are unsure whether a product is appropriate for you, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

01

Read the label. Follow the stated directions, warnings, storage instructions, and expiry information.

02

Use the correct product. Do not assume a medicine is suitable simply because it helped someone else.

03

Ask before combining medicines. A healthcare professional can help identify potential interactions and precautions.

04

Know when to seek medical care. Serious or worsening symptoms require appropriate medical assessment.
